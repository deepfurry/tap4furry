# Tap4Furry — Product Overview

> **A discovery and exchange platform for the furry ecosystem.**

Tap4Furry is a web-first public platform for discovering, understanding,
organizing, contributing, and exchanging information about furry-related resources.

It is intentionally lightweight, clean, fast, and low-maintenance.

The **Resource Graph** is the center of the product. Community, exchange, identity,
polls, and insights exist to strengthen resource discovery and ecosystem knowledge,
not to become independent social products.

## Core User Value

Tap4Furry should help users:

- **Find** furry-related resources quickly.
- **Understand** what a resource is, who created it, where it comes from, and whether it is still available.
- **Organize** resources through Save, Want, Have, and Collections.
- **Contribute** resources, corrections, sources, translations, relations, and metadata.
- **Exchange** availability information through Want / Trade / Offer listings without platform-managed transactions.
- **Observe** the furry ecosystem through public aggregate data and long-term trends.

## Core Principles

1. **Resource First**
2. **Web First**
3. **Read First**
4. **Publish ≠ Distribution**
5. **Resource ≠ Source ≠ Distribution**
6. **Identity without Social Pressure**
7. **Privacy by Default**
8. **Reputation through Evidence**
9. **Contribution over Administration**
10. **Automation by Exception**
11. **Complexity Must Be Earned**
12. **Ecosystem over Surveillance**

## Service Boundary

Tap4Furry does **not** process payments, hold funds, provide escrow, guarantee transactions,
verify delivery, or provide transaction insurance.

Exchange helps users discover and contact one another. Transactions occur directly between users
or through external services.

Tap4Furry is not a general-purpose file host.

## Non-goals

Tap4Furry is not intended to become:

- an e-commerce/payment platform;
- a generic social feed or friend graph;
- a follower-count competition platform;
- a general-purpose real-time chat service;
- an XP / karma / leveling system;
- a creator monetization platform;
- an ad-tech behavioral tracking system;
- a mandatory real-name identity system;
- a generic file-sharing host.
